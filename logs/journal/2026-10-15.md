## Findings and surprises

- On Foundry, **models must be pinned**. Without `ANTHROPIC_DEFAULT_OPUS_MODEL`, `opus` resolves to Opus 4.6.
- Foundry deployments have a **hosting option** (Azure or Anthropic infrastructure). This is a data-residency decision → ADR-009.
- **Server-managed settings and the analytics dashboard aren't available** on Foundry, so measurement relies on OTel + Azure + GitHub.
- Since v2.1.283, **auto mode is the default** starting mode, even on Foundry. The managed `defaultMode` must be set explicitly.
- `allowManagedPermissionRulesOnly` **ignores `--allowedTools`**, so CI allowlists must be in managed policy.
- Commands run with the `!` prefix **run outside Claude's sandbox**. Only the container firewall applies to them.
- Open questions: whether skill/subagent frontmatter hooks are blocked by `allowManagedHooksOnly` (R-21), and whether user-level `excludedCommands` apply under managed policy (R-22).
